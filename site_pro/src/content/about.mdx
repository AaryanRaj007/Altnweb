# about altn LUTs

A set of 33 color grading LUTs built for trekking footage and drone aerials.

Every LUT was authored from scratch on real footage - Himalayan treks shot on iPhone (Apple Log, ProRes 422 HQ) and drone aerials shot on DJI (D-Log). No repacked third-party presets, no licensing ambiguity. If you use these, you're using work that was built by hand on the same kind of footage you're probably editing.

## why this exists

Most LUT packs fall into one of two traps: they're either generic "cinematic looks" built on studio footage that falls apart on outdoor/travel content, or they're tied to a single camera and break when you switch sources.

This pack was built specifically for the footage it grades - outdoor, natural light, mixed altitude, mixed weather. And it handles two camera sources (Apple Log, DJI D-Log) through a utility-layer architecture instead of shipping two versions of every grade.

## how it's built

**Two-layer pipeline.** A utility LUT converts your camera's log format to Rec.709 (standard color space). Every creative LUT then sits on top of that normalized signal. This means 33 creative files work on both camera sources, instead of needing 66.

**65-point precision.** DJI's own reference LUTs ship at 33-point. That's fine for flat scenes, but drone footage is sky-heavy - smooth gradients are where low-resolution LUTs produce visible banding. 65-point eliminates it.

**DaVinci Resolve native.** Standard `.cube` format. Works in Resolve, Premiere, FCP, or anything that reads 3D LUTs. No plugins, no accounts, no subscriptions.
